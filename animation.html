Overview

A charming character comes to life.

A vibrant illustrated animation featuring a charming flower character enjoying a cozy movie night. Built in Illustrator using masking and Shape Builder, then brought to life in After Effects with Puppet Pin and Anchor Point animation. The piece blends a warm, cartoony aesthetic with playful character design, fluid motion, and integrated sound design.
